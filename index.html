About Shaylee

I'm an independent developer, product designer, and digital artist. I take small browser worlds and practical personal tools from idea through interaction design, systems, interface, and development—then carry the same strange-nature instincts into art and reusable resources for other makers.

I also experiment with personal planning in Life-OS and local-first pantry planning in Grocery Brain.
