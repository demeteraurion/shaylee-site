Build belief carefully

VEILBORN is a browser management game about turning a handful of seekers into a durable congregation. Faith, money, devotion, housing, and suspicion all compete for attention while the compound grows around the people living inside it.

Followers have names, ranks, traits, conditions, and assignments. Buildings create passive effects and staffing needs. Campaign events force the congregation to deal with neighbors, authorities, the press, and the consequences of whichever doctrine it chooses.

Inside the compound

  • Named followers with traits, ranks, strain, and assignments
  • Compound construction with adjacency and staffing effects
  • Faith, money, devotion, and suspicion economies
  • Doctrine paths, campaign events, choices, and endings

System notes

Pressure comes from every direction

The management layer is built around tradeoffs rather than a single upgrade path. Growth makes the congregation more capable, more expensive, and more visible.

A compound with jobs to do

Ten buildings range from the first Shrine and Dormitory to staffed ritual spaces, an Offering Pit, and the late-game Veil Spire. Each changes capacity, income, cooldowns, devotion, or exposure.

Three ways to believe

The Hidden Hand favors secrecy, the Golden Gospel turns prosperity into influence, and the Open Eye pursues revelation at the cost of attention. Each doctrine combines a passive benefit, a drawback, and a defining action.

Consequences stay visible

Followers carry rank, traits, strain, and assignments while actions reshape faith, money, devotion, and suspicion. The interface is being tuned so gains and costs are understandable before they become emergencies.
