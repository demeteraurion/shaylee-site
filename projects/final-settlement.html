A missing arrival

The dead are expected to cross over. This one did not. Final Settlement follows a private detective through records, testimony, debts, and the uncomfortable spaces between death and whatever comes next.

The project brings together investigation, document-driven clues, and a world built around bureaucratic supernatural rules. It is designed to be played directly in the browser.

Inside the case

  • Point-and-click investigation
  • Noir atmosphere and supernatural systems
  • Evidence, dialogue, and deduction
