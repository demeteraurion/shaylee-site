There is more below

Below the Known is an in-development deep-sea game built around curiosity under pressure. From the safety of a small submersible, the player will push into a place that is beautiful, hostile, and only partly understood.

The signal

  • Deep-sea observation and exploration
  • Photography as a way to document the unknown
  • Survival under the pressure of an unforgiving descent
  • A setting shaped by the boundary between discovery and danger

Project status

Below the Known is in early development. More details will surface as the expedition takes shape.
