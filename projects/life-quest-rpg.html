From the notice board to the road

You arrive in Alderwick as a new adventurer. At the Wayfarer’s Rest, the notices might send you looking for a missing animal, gathering supplies, or checking why a light keeps appearing in an empty house.

Pick a notice, choose up to two companions, and pack for the journey. Expeditions run on a timer, so you can step away and come back later. Some journeys pause for an encounter where you decide what the party does. When they return, the Journal keeps the account of what happened and what you discovered.

Alderwick remembers

Trade has slowed, families have moved away, and some houses stand empty. Harlan still keeps the inn open. In The Houses That Stay Lit, his concern about candlelight in Wick House turns into a five-part investigation into the people who lived there and why someone might still be tending the place.

Life between expeditions

There’s work to do and company to keep even when the party is back at the inn.

Get to know your companions

Travelers come and go. Shared journeys and conversations build familiarity, opening personal quests and optional friendship scenes. The People Journal keeps track of who you’ve met, where you’ve been together, and the memories you’ve earned.

Gather, craft, and cook

Take a paid job, fish, forage, mine, or cut wood. Bring materials to Rook’s Workshop or cook at Harlan’s Hearth. A meal can be a gift for a companion or a provision for the next expedition.

Leave a mark on Alderwick

As your reputation grows, you can help restore the Workshop, contribute supplies to the village fountain, or help Zev open the library. At the Rest, you can decorate the attic guest room with things you’ve made.
