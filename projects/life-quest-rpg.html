Between expeditions at the Wayfarer’s Rest

Read notices, choose who to bring, and pack for the trip. Expeditions unfold over time, so you can step away and return for the outcome.

The inn gives each trip a starting point

The quest board and recruitable travelers share the same room. You can read a notice, see who’s around, and build a party without leaving the tavern.

Back at the inn, get to know your companions and record shared memories in the Journal. Cook a meal at Harlan’s Hearth, then give it to a companion or use it to prepare for a journey.
