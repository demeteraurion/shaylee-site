Read between the records

The Ashgrove Ritual explored investigation through case files, reports, timelines, journals, evidence, and suspect dossiers. The mystery lived in contradictions and in the details that official records almost managed to hide.

Its interface treated documents as the world itself: the player moved through the investigation by reading, connecting, and reconsidering what each source claimed.

What the experiment explored

  • Document-led investigation
  • Occult clues and ritual logic
  • Locked-room mystery structure
  • Case-file interface design
This project is shelved and is no longer available to play.
