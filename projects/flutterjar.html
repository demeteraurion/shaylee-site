Gentle care, not another checklist

Flutterjar is a pocket ecosystem meant to be checked on rather than optimized. Bugs wander, forage, rest, and react to the state of their home while the player tends moisture, food, and small comforts.

The systems reward consistent care without demanding constant attention. Progress saves in the browser, and a Bugpedia adds real-world facts alongside the tiny-world fiction.

Inside the jar

  • Bug behaviors and changing moods
  • Feeding, misting, foraging, and upgrades
  • Day and night lighting
  • No fail states or dark patterns
